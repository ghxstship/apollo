RULES
Billing tiers scale 100 / 58 / 38%. Never two tiers at the same size — that is what makes it a bill and not a list. Small crops drop tiers from the bottom up; they never compress the ratio. One accent fill per flyer. Everything else is ivory, ink, or the scene. Scene grain is always on. A flat gradient with no grain is not a house flyer. Partner marks sit in the footer at 62% opacity, never above the billing block. No urgency copy, no exclamation marks, no year in the date line.
