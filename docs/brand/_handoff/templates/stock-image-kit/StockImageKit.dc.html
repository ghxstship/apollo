SOURCING BRIEF
Minimum 4000px on the long edge — the grade exaggerates softness, and 24×36 needs 300 dpi at full size. Standard licence, no editorial-only frames. Extended licence for anything printed above 500 units. No recognisable faces in stock. A stock face standing in for a member is a consent problem, not an art-direction one. Test every candidate under both treatments before licensing. Frames that only survive one go in the category they survive. Record the Adobe ID in the filename caption here on acquisition. An unattributed frame cannot be re-licensed later.
