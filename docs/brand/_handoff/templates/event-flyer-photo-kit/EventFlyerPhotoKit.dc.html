Overlay is the whole trick. A flat magenta fill at any opacity flattens the water; overlay at 0.34 pushes the highlights warm and the shadows violet while leaving midtone detail — sand still reads as sand. Below 0.28 the sky stops registering as graded; above 0.42 the sea turns to poster paint.

Choosing a photoTHESE RULES GOVERN THE GRADED VARIANT · THE GRADE CANNOT RESCUE A BAD FRAME · MONO HAS ITS OWN RULES, AND ACCEPTS MOST OF WHAT THE GRADE REJECTS
GRADEFRAME YESAerials and wides — the grade needs sky or open water to put the sun in. YESA clear horizon line in the upper third, so the amber band lands on sky. YESMidtone-rich frames. Overlay works on midtones; a blown-out frame has none. NOTight portraits — magenta on skin at 0.34 reads as sunburn. NONight frames — there is no highlight for the overlay to warm. NOFrames already graded warm — double-warming turns the sea orange.
MONO · DIFFERENT CRITERIA ENTIRELY
YESContrast and shape over colour — mono reads structure, not hue. YESNight frames and hard flash — the grade's two worst cases are mono's best. YESTight portraits — no magenta on skin, so faces are back in play. YESAlready-warm stock — desaturation makes the original grade irrelevant. NOLow-contrast or hazy frames — with hue gone, flat tonality has nothing left to carry it.
WHICH VARIANT
Photo, graded when the location sells the event — sandbar, charter, a venue anyone would recognise — and the frame suits the grade. Photo, mono when the photo is strong but off-palette, when the frame fails the graded rules below, or when several divisions post in one week and the accent alone must tell them apart. Gradient when there is no photograph yet, or the billing is the whole story. Never mix the three in one campaign. A season runs on one treatment.
Specimens use licensed stock as stand-ins. Replace with real event photography before a season ships.

RULES
Photo: grayscale 1, contrast 1.14, brightness 0.92. No grade layers — the outrun gradient and sun glow are omitted entirely. Grain lifts to 0.60. With no colour in the frame, grain is what stops the photo reading as flat stock. Type: headliner Neon Canvas, support in the division accent, date line in the -lift step so it holds against grey. Suffix takes the accent here — the one place the lockup carries colour, since the ground cannot. Mono accepts frames the grade rejects: night, tight portraits, and already-warm stock all work here.
