OPTION 1 · ACTIVE RULETAGLINE MASTER
[un]anything goes here
Bracket authority against a literalised fill-in-the-blank. The rule runs the exact length of the phrase and nothing else — it is a blank waiting to be filled, not an underline for emphasis.
ANCHORAnton, uppercase, crisp edges. TAGLINESpace Mono lowercase at 0.65 of the bracket size, on a continuous 1.5 pt rule the exact length of the phrase. Deliberately below the cap-matched 1.27 used for division suffixes — the tagline is a caption under the mark, not a second half of it. CLEARANCERule sits 1–2 px clear of the descenders on g and y — 0.06em, never less than 1 px. It must never cut the letterforms when scaled down or embroidered. SPACINGOne full character space between ] and anything — .61em of the tagline size, one Space Mono advance. USEMerch back-prints on washed cotton, web hero banners under the nav, VIP dock-bag welcome cards, marina passes, invitation envelopes, cocktail napkins, email sign-offs. AVOIDApp icons and avatars — the long rule and small script break at micro-scale, use the 1:1 anchor. Embroidery under 25 mm — thread fuses the rule into the descenders of g and y. Co-branded sponsor lockups — the rule clutters against partner marks.
OPTION 3 · ACTIVE CURSORDIGITAL & MOTION
[un]anything
goes here
A live prompt. Reads as a terminal waiting for input, which is exactly what the brand is — the cursor implies the next word is already being typed.
ANCHORAnton, uppercase. CURSORThin vertical rule at 50% opacity in Golden Sand #E6C687, .61em of the tagline size each side. TAGLINESpace Mono lowercase, .05em tracking, stacked two lines so the block sits square beside the anchor — a single line is too wide for mobile and video. MOTIONPipe blinks on a 0.8 s loop at terminal speed, then the phrase auto-deletes and types a division name. USESocial video overlays where the phrase backspaces and retypes into Hinged, Bound, Limited. Posh headers, wallet pass sub-headers, nav hover and loading states, marina and DJ-booth signage. AVOIDApparel embroidery — the pipe reads as a seam flaw or stray thread. Laser-engraved metal — vertical lines etch like scratches. Print billboards — the digital metaphor has no context offline.
