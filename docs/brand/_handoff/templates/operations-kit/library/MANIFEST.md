## Read this before distributing anything

**`06-safety-and-compliance.md` is not a guest document.** It contains crew testing procedures, incident escalation, and instructions on what crew must not say after a casualty. Distribute to crew and management only.

**`01-guest-policy-suite.md` contains an internal operator note** headed *NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION*, plus the roadmap appendix. Use `PUBLIC-READY/guest-policy-suite-PUBLIC.md` for anything guest-facing, or delete those two sections yourself before publishing.

**Three unresolved decisions block publication:**

1. **The alcohol model.** File 05 contains three complete, mutually exclusive policies — licensed vendor service, BYOB, or dry. Choose one with counsel, delete the other two, then set every `[ALCOHOL MODEL]` marker across the library to match. Florida's special excursion boat permit under § 565.02(3)(a) requires 125-passenger USCG capacity, which this vessel cannot reach, so the obvious route is closed.
2. **Minors.** File 02 § 3.1 offers three options. The rest of the library branches from that choice.
3. **Gratuity.** File 05 § 3.1 offers three options.

**Sections requiring information I don't have:**
- File 04 § 1.1 — accessibility matrix. Needs a physical walkthrough of the vessel and gangway. Do not publish assumed dimensions.
- File 06 § 4.1 — station bill crew duty assignments.
- File 08 § 4.9 — environmental commitments. Deliberately blank; publish only what you can verify.

**Three figures to confirm with USCG Sector Miami Prevention:**
- Current property-damage dollar threshold for CG-2692 casualty reporting (amended since original rulemaking)
- Current federal random drug testing rate under 46 CFR Part 16 (set annually)
- Exact pin cite for the passenger count requirement in 46 CFR Part 185

## Standing caveats

**This is a drafting aid, not legal advice.** Files 02, 05, and 07 contain contract, release, licensing, and privacy language with real legal consequence. A Florida-licensed maritime attorney and your insurance carrier must both review before any of it reaches a guest. Florida construes exculpatory clauses narrowly and against the drafter, and releases purporting to waive gross negligence are unenforceable.

**The Certificate of Inspection governs.** Every capacity, crew, route, and equipment reference must be reconciled against the vessel's actual COI. Where this library and the COI disagree, the COI is right and this library is wrong.

**Subchapter T applies.** Under 46 CFR § 175.110, carrying more than six passengers including at least one for hire requires a COI and a credentialed Master. Membership dues are generally consideration. Florida's Boater Freedom Act (SB 1388, effective July 1, 2025) restricts FWC stops of *recreational* vessels and offers this operation no shelter — the Coast Guard may board a commercial passenger vessel at any time.

**Review cycle:** annually, and after any incident, COI change, or regulatory amendment.
