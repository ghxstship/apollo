# 1 · PHOTO, VIDEO & MEDIA RELEASE

## 1.1 What we capture, and why

[UN] Hinged Social photographs and records aboard M/V Sea Charm II and at associated events. The material is used for `[social media, website, marketing, membership recruitment, partner and sponsor reporting, and archival]`.

We tell you this at booking, we tell you again at check-in, and we make opting out easy — because a members' club runs on people feeling comfortable, and being photographed without warning is the fastest way to lose that.

## 1.2 The release

By booking and boarding, you grant [UN] Hinged Social a `[non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual]` license to photograph, film, and record you, and to reproduce, edit, publish, and distribute that material in any medium for the purposes described above.

You waive any right to inspect or approve the finished material, and any claim for compensation. You acknowledge that material published online cannot be fully recalled.

> `ATTORNEY:` Fla. Stat. § 540.08 requires express written or oral consent for commercial use of a person's name or likeness. Confirm the booking-flow acceptance mechanism satisfies "express consent" for your intended uses, particularly paid advertising and sponsor deliverables.

## 1.3 Opting out

**You can decline. It costs you nothing and no one will make it awkward.**

| How | When |
|---|---|
| Check the media opt-out box at booking | Any time before sailing |
| Tell staff at check-in | Boarding |
| Request a visual opt-out marker `[wristband / lanyard / pin]` | Boarding |
| Email `[CLUB EMAIL]` | Any time |

Opted-out guests are excluded from posed and featured content. **We cannot guarantee exclusion from wide crowd shots** taken on a `[45-foot]` deck — there is nowhere to stand that is genuinely out of frame. If you appear in published material and want it removed, email `[CLUB EMAIL]` and we will remove it from Company-controlled channels within `[10]` business days.

## 1.4 Removal requests

We honor removal requests for Company-controlled channels without requiring a reason. We cannot remove material from third-party reposts, press coverage, guest accounts, or partner channels — and we will tell you honestly when that is the situation rather than promising a takedown we can't deliver.

## 1.5 What we will not publish

- Images that are unflattering in a way we wouldn't want published of ourselves
- Anyone who has opted out, in posed or featured content
- Minors, without separate written parental consent `[if minors sail at all]`
- Images that reveal a guest's medical condition, disability, or a private moment
- Anyone visibly intoxicated, distressed, or in an emergency or medical situation
- Guests in states of undress beyond ordinary swimwear
- Content from an incident, casualty, or emergency
- Identifying details — full names, employers, or locations — without separate permission

## 1.6 Guest photography

Welcome, for personal use, subject to the Code of Conduct.

**Not welcome:** photographing people who have declined or are wearing an opt-out marker · recording crew or other guests for harassment · any recording into changing areas or up clothing · commercial or press use without written media authorization · publishing identifiable images of other guests without their agreement.

**Ask before you post someone else.** It is a small deck and everyone will meet again.

## 1.7 Professional and press media

Professional crews, tripods, gimbals over `[24"]`, drones (prohibited outright), and press attendance require prior written authorization from `[MEDIA CONTACT]`, a signed media agreement, and proof of insurance. Working media are escorted and identifiable.

## 1.8 Talent and vendors

Performers, DJs, and vendors are covered by their own agreements. See the Talent, Vendor & Contractor Conduct Rider.

## 1.9 Tagging and credit

Guests are welcome to tag `[HANDLES]`. When we repost guest content we credit the creator. We seek permission before using guest content in paid advertising.
