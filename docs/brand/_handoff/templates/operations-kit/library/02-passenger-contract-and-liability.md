# 1 · TERMS OF CARRIAGE

**The passenger contract for travel aboard M/V Sea Charm II**

By booking, boarding, or accepting a berth aboard M/V Sea Charm II, you enter into a contract with [un] Hinged Social, a `[STATE OF FORMATION]` limited liability company ("the Company"). Read it. It governs your rights.

## 1.1 What you're buying

A berth aboard a scheduled club sailing. You are purchasing **passage on a specific sailing, at a specific time, on a specific vessel** — not a private charter, not exclusive use, not a guaranteed itinerary, and not a guaranteed destination.

## 1.2 The Master's authority governs everything

The Master holds full legal authority over the vessel and every person aboard. The Master may alter the route, shorten or extend the voyage, cancel a swim call, change or omit a destination, refuse boarding, remove a passenger, or return to dock at any time for any reason relating to safety, weather, mechanical condition, traffic, regulatory direction, or passenger conduct. **No such decision creates a right to refund, compensation, or rebooking except as stated in the Weather & Cancellation Policy.**

## 1.3 Itineraries are intentions

Published destinations, anchorages, sandbar stops, timing, and durations are estimates. Tides, bridge schedules, weather, sea state, marine traffic, water quality, wildlife closures, and law enforcement direction routinely change them. Deviation is not a breach of this contract.

## 1.4 Capacity is absolute

Boarding is limited by the vessel's U.S. Coast Guard Certificate of Inspection. Once capacity is reached, no additional person boards for any reason. Holding a reservation does not override the COI.

## 1.5 Your obligations as a passenger

You agree to:
- Present valid government-issued photo identification at check-in
- Attend the pre-departure safety orientation in full
- Comply immediately with all instructions from the Master and crew
- Comply with the Code of Conduct, Prohibited Items list, and Zero Tolerance Policies, all incorporated into this contract by reference
- Disclose any medical condition, pregnancy, mobility limitation, or non-swimmer status that bears on your safety aboard
- Refrain from boarding if you are impaired, ill, or contagious
- Accept bag inspection as a condition of boarding

## 1.6 Removal and refusal

The Company may refuse boarding or remove you at the next safe opportunity for any violation of these terms. **Removal forfeits the entire fare.** Removal offshore occurs only at a safe location; you are responsible for your own transport from the point of disembarkation.

## 1.7 Assumption of risk

Marine passenger transport carries inherent risks that cannot be eliminated: vessel motion, wet and slippery decks, sun and heat exposure, weather, drowning, marine life, collision, fire, mechanical failure, and the acts of other passengers. **By boarding, you knowingly and voluntarily assume these risks.** See the separate Liability Waiver & Assumption of Risk.

## 1.8 Limitation of liability

`ATTORNEY — this clause is the most consequential in the document.`

The Company's liability is limited to the maximum extent permitted by applicable federal maritime law and Florida law. The Company is not liable for:
- Indirect, incidental, consequential, punitive, or exemplary damages
- Loss, theft, or damage to personal property (see § 5, Personal Property)
- Delay, missed connections, or missed subsequent plans
- Acts of third parties including other passengers, vendors, and independent contractors
- Force majeure events

**Notice of claim and time to sue.** Any claim for personal injury or death must be notified in writing to the Company within `[6 MONTHS]` of the incident, and any suit must be filed within `[1 YEAR]`.

> `ATTORNEY:` 46 U.S.C. § 30508 permits these shortened periods for **seagoing vessels**, but expressly excludes certain vessels operating on inland waters and rivers. Whether a 45-foot pontoon operating in Biscayne Bay and the Intracoastal qualifies as "seagoing" is a live question, and it determines whether this clause is enforceable at all. It also requires conspicuous disclosure in the ticket — booking-flow placement matters as much as wording. Resolve both before publication.

## 1.9 Forum and governing law

This contract is governed by the general maritime law of the United States and, where maritime law does not apply, the law of the State of Florida. Any action must be brought exclusively in `[FORUM — federal court, Southern District of Florida, or state court in Miami-Dade County]`. `ATTORNEY — forum selection clauses in passenger tickets are generally enforceable but require conspicuous notice.`

## 1.10 Tickets and transfers

Berths are issued to a named individual. Transfer is permitted only through the terms in the No-Show, Transfer & Waitlist Policy. Resale for profit is prohibited and voids the berth without refund.

## 1.11 Photography

The Company records aboard for promotional use. See the Photo, Video & Media Release.

## 1.12 Incorporated documents

The following are incorporated into this contract in full: Code of Conduct · Prohibited Items · Allowed Items · Zero Tolerance Policies · Emergency Procedures · Weather, Cancellation & Refund Policy · Boarding, Screening & Right of Refusal Policy · Alcohol Service Policy · Privacy Policy · Photo & Video Release · Liability Waiver.

## 1.13 Amendment and severability

The Company may amend these terms; the version published at the time of your booking governs your sailing. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder stands.

# 3 · MINOR POLICY & PARENTAL CONSENT

## 3.1 Are minors permitted aboard?

`[CHOOSE ONE AND DELETE THE OTHERS]`
- [ ] **Adults only (21+).** No guest under 21 may board any sailing.
- [ ] **Adults only (18+).** No guest under 18 may board any sailing.
- [ ] **Family sailings only.** Minors permitted on designated family sailings, never on `[ADULT-DESIGNATED]` sailings.

> **Recommendation:** For an alcohol-forward evening social series, a hard 21+ standard is dramatically simpler to administer and eliminates most of your dram shop, supervision, and consent exposure at a stroke. If you want a family product, run it as a separate, clearly branded daytime series with its own rules rather than mixing populations on one deck.

## 3.2 Where minors are permitted

**No unaccompanied minors.** Every guest under 18 must be accompanied for the entire sailing by a parent or legal guardian who is also aboard. One adult may supervise no more than `[2]` minors.

**Mandatory life jacket wear.** Every guest under `[13]` wears a properly fitted, USCG-approved personal flotation device **at all times while aboard**, not merely while underway. This is a club standard that exceeds the Florida statutory minimum, and it is not waivable by a parent.

**Swim calls.** Minors enter the water only with their accompanying adult physically in the water alongside them. No exceptions, no "I'll watch from the deck."

**Alcohol.** No guest under 21 may possess or consume alcohol. Furnishing alcohol to a minor is a felony under Fla. Stat. § 562.11 and a zero-tolerance violation resulting in removal of both the minor and the supplying adult.

## 3.3 Parental consent and release

A parent or legal guardian must execute the Liability Waiver on the minor's behalf and provide proof of guardianship on request.

> `ATTORNEY:` Fla. Stat. § 744.301(3) permits a parent to execute a pre-injury release on a minor's behalf for **inherent risks** of an activity, subject to statutory notice requirements and specific formatting. The statute's protections are narrow and procedurally exacting. Counsel must draft this release to the statute or it will not hold.

## 3.4 Identification

Accompanying adults must present ID establishing their own identity. The Company may request documentation of the guardian relationship.

## 3.5 Conduct

Accompanying adults are fully responsible for the conduct, supervision, and safety of minors in their care. Failure to supervise is grounds for removal of the entire party.

# 4 · AGE VERIFICATION & IDENTIFICATION POLICY

## 4.1 The standard

**Valid, unexpired, government-issued photo identification is required from every guest at check-in, every sailing, with no exceptions for members, repeat guests, staff friends, or anyone the crew personally recognizes.**

The exception you make once becomes the practice you can't defend later.

## 4.2 Accepted identification

- U.S. state driver's license or state ID card
- U.S. passport or passport card
- U.S. military ID
- Permanent resident card
- Foreign passport
- Foreign driver's license accompanied by a passport

## 4.3 Not accepted

Photocopies, photographs of an ID, expired documents, temporary paper licenses without accompanying photo ID, school IDs, work badges, digital wallet IDs `[SET POLICY]`, and any ID that appears altered, damaged, or does not match the bearer.

## 4.4 Verification points

| Stage | Check |
|---|---|
| Booking | Date of birth captured, attested, and age-gated |
| Check-in | Physical ID inspected against booking name and face |
| Alcohol service | ID may be requested again at any point by any crew member |
| Wristbanding | Age-differentiated wristband applied at check-in `[IF MIXED-AGE SAILINGS]` |

## 4.5 Refusal

A guest who cannot produce acceptable ID does not board and does not receive a refund. This is stated at booking so it cannot be disputed at the gangway.

## 4.6 False identification

Presenting false, altered, or borrowed ID is a Zero Tolerance violation. The document is retained where lawful, the guest is refused, and the matter may be referred to law enforcement.

## 4.7 Wristbanding

Where sailings are mixed-age, a tamper-evident wristband is applied at check-in and must remain on the wrist for the duration. Transferring, removing, or altering a wristband is a Zero Tolerance violation.

## 4.8 Privacy

ID is inspected visually. The Company does not photograph, scan, or retain copies of identification documents except where required by law. See the Privacy Policy.

# 5 · LOST & FOUND AND PERSONAL PROPERTY POLICY

## 5.1 The short version

**Bring nothing aboard you would grieve.** There is no secure storage on this vessel. There are no lockers, no safe, and no bag check. Decks are wet, the boat moves, and things go overboard with a finality that surprises people.

## 5.2 Liability

[un] Hinged Social is not responsible for loss, theft, damage, or overboard loss of any personal property brought aboard or left at the dock facility, including phones, jewelry, sunglasses, watches, cameras, keys, wallets, and clothing. Property is carried entirely at the owner's risk.

## 5.3 What we recommend

- Tether or float your phone. A floating waterproof pouch costs almost nothing and is the single highest-return item you can bring.
- Leave jewelry ashore. Cold water shrinks fingers and rings disappear.
- Sunglasses on a retainer.
- One key, not a keyring.
- Photograph the contents of your bag before you board.

## 5.5 Items lost overboard

Not recoverable. The Company does not conduct dives, searches, or salvage for personal property, and will not delay or divert a sailing to attempt recovery. The Master may attempt a surface recovery only if it is safe and does not compromise the voyage.

## 5.6 Property left at the dock

The dock facility is operated by `[MARINA]` and is not under the Company's control. The Company has no responsibility for vehicles or property left in the parking area.

## 5.7 Confiscated and refused items

Items refused at screening are **not stored, transported, or held.** The guest must return them to their vehicle or forfeit them. Prohibited items surrendered at the gangway are not returned and may be disposed of or turned over to law enforcement where illegal.
