# 1 · WEATHER, CANCELLATION, RESCHEDULING & REFUND POLICY

South Florida weather is not a footnote to this operation — it is the primary variable. This policy exists so that cancellation decisions are made against published, objective criteria rather than argued at the dock with forty people watching.

## 1.1 Who decides, and when

The **Master alone** decides whether a sailing proceeds. The decision is made on the basis of NWS Miami marine forecasts, live radar, observed conditions, and the Master's professional judgment. It is final and not subject to guest input, vote, or appeal.

**Decision timeline:**

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| 24 hours prior | Preliminary forecast review; guests notified if outlook is marginal |
| 4 hours prior | Go / no-go decision; all guests notified by `[SMS + EMAIL]` |
| At the dock | Final call; conditions can change in twenty minutes here |
| Underway | Master may curtail at any point |

## 1.2 Objective cancellation triggers

The Company cancels or curtails when any of the following exists or is forecast for the sailing window:

- **Small Craft Advisory** in effect for Biscayne Bay or coastal waters `[ADJUST TO ROUTE]`
- Sustained winds above **`[20]` knots** or gusts above **`[25]` knots**
- Seas above **`[3]` feet** in the operating area
- **Lightning within `[10]` statute miles** of the route or anchorage
- Tropical storm or hurricane watch or warning for Miami-Dade or Broward
- Dense fog or visibility below **`[1]` nautical mile**
- Coast Guard port condition restrictions, security zone activation, or waterway closure
- Health advisory, algal bloom, or water quality closure affecting the anchorage
- Mechanical condition affecting the vessel's certificate or safe operation
- Insufficient credentialed crew to satisfy the COI manning requirement

**Marginal conditions.** A sailing may proceed with the swim call cancelled, the route shortened, or the anchorage changed. This is a modification, not a cancellation, and does not trigger a refund.

## 1.3 Refund matrix

| Scenario | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Company cancels before departure** (weather, mechanical, regulatory, crew) | Full refund **or** transferable credit valid `[12 months]` — guest's choice |
| **Company curtails after departure**, under 50% of scheduled duration completed | `[50%]` credit |
| **Company curtails after departure**, 50% or more completed | No refund |
| **Swim call cancelled**, sailing otherwise proceeds | No refund |
| **Route or destination changed** | No refund |
| **Guest cancels 15+ days out** | Full refund less `[processing fee]` |
| **Guest cancels 7–14 days out** | `[50%]` refund or full credit |
| **Guest cancels under 7 days** | No refund; berth may be transferred per the Transfer Policy |
| **Guest no-show** | No refund |
| **Guest denied boarding** (ID, impairment, prohibited item, capacity, conduct) | No refund |
| **Guest removed for conduct** | No refund |
| **Guest illness with documentation** | Credit at the Company's discretion |

## 1.4 Hurricane season

June 1 through November 30. Sailings within a **named storm's forecast cone** for Miami-Dade or Broward are cancelled with full refund or credit, regardless of conditions on the day. The Company will not attempt to thread a storm.

## 1.5 Force majeure

The Company is not liable for failure to perform due to weather, act of God, government action, port closure, civil unrest, public health emergency, labor action, marina closure, utility failure, or any cause beyond reasonable control. Remedies in these cases are limited to the refund matrix above.

## 1.6 How refunds are processed

To the original payment method, within `[10]` business days. Credits are issued as a code tied to the member account, transferable within the membership `[YES / NO]`.

## 1.7 What we will not do

- Sail into conditions the Master judges unsafe because guests have travelled, paid, or are disappointed
- Reimburse travel, lodging, parking, childcare, or other consequential costs arising from a cancellation
- Hold a sailing for late guests

# 2 · CAPACITY & MANIFEST POLICY

## 2.1 The hard ceiling

The vessel's **U.S. Coast Guard Certificate of Inspection sets the maximum number of persons aboard: `[COI CAPACITY]` passengers plus `[N]` crew.** This is a federal legal limit. It is not a soft target, a guideline, or a number that flexes for a member's plus-one, a photographer, a DJ, or the owner's friend.

**Every person aboard counts** — guests, members, staff not on the crew complement, talent, vendors, photographers, and children of any age.

## 2.2 Booking against capacity

Berths released for sale = COI passenger capacity **minus** a reserved allocation of `[N]` for:
- Non-crew staff and hosts
- Talent and performers
- Media and content capture
- Contingency

Overbooking is prohibited. The Company does not sell berths it cannot honor.

## 2.3 Passenger count and manifest

The Master is required under 46 CFR Part 185 to **keep a correct written count of all passengers embarking and disembarking, and to communicate that count so it is available ashore before departure.** `[VERIFY PIN CITE WITH OCMI]`

Operational standard:

| Point | Action | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| Check-in | Name checked against booking, ID verified, count incremented | `[CHECK-IN LEAD]` |
| Pre-departure | Final count taken and read back to Master | `[CHECK-IN LEAD]` |
| Pre-departure | Count and manifest transmitted to shoreside contact | `[SHORESIDE CONTACT]` |
| Before swim call | Head count | Deck crew |
| After swim call | Head count, reconciled to pre-call number | Deck crew |
| Before departing any stop | Head count | Deck crew |
| At return | Disembark count, reconciled | `[CHECK-IN LEAD]` |

**A discrepancy at any count stops the vessel.** The boat does not get underway, does not leave an anchorage, and does not depart a stop on an unreconciled count.

## 2.4 Manifest contents

For each person aboard: full name, date of birth, mobile number, emergency contact name and number, and disclosed medical or non-swimmer flags. Retained per the Privacy Policy and made available to the Coast Guard or emergency responders on request.

## 2.5 Shoreside copy

A copy of the manifest and passenger count is held ashore by `[SHORESIDE CONTACT]` for the duration of every sailing. This is what makes a search and rescue response effective, and it is the difference between "we think there were about forty" and a name-by-name accounting.

## 2.6 When capacity is reached

Boarding stops. Full stop. Guests with valid reservations who could not board because of a Company error receive a full refund plus a `[complimentary berth]` on a future sailing. Guests who arrived after check-in closed receive nothing.

## 2.7 Weight and distribution

Beyond headcount, the Master may direct seating and movement to manage stability and trim. Comply immediately. On a pontoon platform, passenger distribution is a live stability factor, not a comfort preference.

# 3 · BOARDING, SCREENING & RIGHT OF REFUSAL POLICY

**Published in advance because a screening policy that appears for the first time at the gangway is not enforceable and is not fair.**

## 3.1 Check-in window

Opens `[60]` minutes before scheduled departure. **Closes `[15]` minutes before departure.** Guests arriving after close do not board and do not receive a refund. The vessel departs on schedule.

## 3.2 The boarding sequence

1. **Identification.** Photo ID inspected and matched to the reservation.
2. **Manifest.** Name confirmed, emergency contact captured, medical and non-swimmer flags noted.
3. **Waiver.** Executed if not already completed digitally.
4. **Wristband.** Applied if the sailing is mixed-age or wristbanded.
5. **Bag inspection.** Every bag, every guest, every sailing.
6. **Fitness observation.** Crew assess apparent impairment and fitness to board.
7. **Safety orientation.** Mandatory, in full, before getting underway.

## 3.3 Bag inspection

All bags, coolers, and containers are subject to visual inspection. Guests open their own bags; crew do not search persons. **Refusing inspection means refusing boarding, with no refund.** Prohibited items must be returned to your vehicle or surrendered — the Company does not store them.

## 3.4 Grounds for denied boarding

The Company may refuse boarding to any person who:

- Cannot produce acceptable identification
- Appears impaired by alcohol or any substance
- Is unable to walk the gangway or board unassisted, where no accommodation has been arranged in advance
- Refuses bag inspection
- Possesses a prohibited item and will not surrender it
- Is under the applicable minimum age for the sailing
- Behaves aggressively, abusively, or threateningly toward crew, staff, or other guests at the dock
- Refuses to attend or disrupts the safety orientation
- Is subject to an active suspension or revocation
- Appears acutely ill or contagious
- Would exceed the COI capacity
- Presents any other condition the Master judges a risk to the safe operation of the vessel

## 3.5 Impairment assessment

Crew observe rather than test. Indicators include unsteady gait, slurred speech, inability to follow simple instruction, disorientation, and the accounts of companions. **Where crew are uncertain, the guest does not sail.** A disappointed guest ashore is an entirely recoverable situation. An impaired guest offshore is not.

Crew making a refusal are supported by management without exception. There is no scenario in which a refusal is later overturned in front of the guest.

## 3.6 How refusals are handled

Refusals are delivered privately where possible, briefly, and without argument or debate. Crew state the decision, not a justification. The decision is final at the dock.

## 3.7 Right of review

A refused guest may submit a written review request to `[CLUB EMAIL]` within 14 days. Review addresses membership standing and future eligibility only — it does not produce a refund of the refused sailing.

## 3.8 Non-discrimination

Refusals are made on the grounds listed in § 3.4 and nowhere else. See the Non-Discrimination Policy. Any guest who believes a refusal was discriminatory should contact `[SAFETY OFFICER]` directly.

## 3.9 Records

Every refusal is logged: date, sailing, guest, grounds, crew member, and witness. Retained for `[3 years]`.

# 4 · NO-SHOW, TRANSFER & WAITLIST POLICY

**Fixed capacity makes an empty berth expensive and a wasted berth unforgivable.**

## 4.1 No-shows

A booked guest who does not check in before the window closes forfeits the berth and the full fare. Berths are not held.

**Repeat no-shows:**

| Occurrence | Consequence |
|---|---|
| First | Logged, no action |
| Second within `[12 months]` | Written notice |
| Third within `[12 months]` | `[90-day]` booking suspension |
| Fourth | Membership review |

## 4.2 Transfers

Berths may be transferred to another eligible person up to **`[48]` hours before departure**, at no charge, via `[CLUB EMAIL]` or the member portal.

Conditions:
- The recipient must meet all eligibility requirements, including age and membership standing
- The recipient must complete the manifest, waiver, and ID verification in their own name
- The original member remains responsible for the recipient's conduct under the Member Sponsorship Policy
- **Transfer for profit is prohibited** and voids the berth without refund
- Transfers are not permitted to any person under suspension or revocation

Inside 48 hours, transfers are handled through the waitlist rather than privately, so the manifest stays accurate.

## 4.3 Waitlist

**How it works:** Join at no cost. Positions are allocated in order of joining, not by membership tier. `[ADJUST IF TIERED]`

**Release process:**

| Timing | Notification | Response window |
|---|---|---|
| More than 48 hours out | `[SMS + EMAIL]` | `[12]` hours |
| 48 to 12 hours out | `[SMS]` | `[2]` hours |
| Under 12 hours | `[SMS]` | `[30]` minutes |

Non-response forfeits the offer and passes it to the next position. Declining does not lose your position for future sailings; three consecutive non-responses removes you from the list for that season.

## 4.4 Same-day standby

At the Company's discretion, unclaimed berths may be released to standby guests present at the dock at check-in close. Standby is not a reservation, carries no priority, and requires full completion of check-in in the remaining window.

## 4.5 Cancelling with notice

Guests who cancel with genuine notice free a berth that can be resold and are treated accordingly under the refund matrix. Guests who simply don't appear are not. The policy is designed to reward the message you send at 9 AM over the silence at 6 PM.
