## Standing caveats that apply to every document in this library

**1. This is a drafting aid, not legal advice.** I am not a lawyer. Files 02 and 07 in particular contain contract and release language that carries real legal consequence and **must** be reviewed by a Florida-licensed maritime attorney before use. Several provisions are marked `ATTORNEY` where the correct answer depends on facts I can't resolve.

**2. The COI governs.** Every capacity, crew, route, and equipment reference must be reconciled against the vessel's actual Certificate of Inspection. Where this library and the COI disagree, the COI wins and this library is wrong.

**3. The alcohol model is unresolved.** Florida's special excursion boat permit under § 565.02(3)(a) requires USCG-approved capacity of at least 125 passengers, which the 4512 cannot reach. Everything marked `[ALCOHOL MODEL]` depends on choosing among licensed vendor service, BYOB, or dry.

**4. Subchapter T applies.** Under 46 CFR § 175.110 this is an inspected small passenger vessel operation carrying passengers for hire. Florida's Boater Freedom Act (SB 1388, 2025) restricts FWC stops of *recreational* vessels and provides no shelter here.
