If the user invokes this skill without other guidance, ask what they want to build, ask some questions, and act as an expert designer who outputs HTML artifacts _or_ production code, depending on the need.

**Brand:** `[UN]` is the parent brand — a static bracketed anchor, brackets never dropped. Six divisions add a sentence-case suffix one space after the bracket: [UN] Hinged (Singles Social Club), [UN] Bound (Alternative Lifestyle Social Club), [UN] Limited (Premium Social Club), [UN] Scripted (pop-up dating and mixers), [UN] Cut (members-only media), [UN] Brand (nautical lifestyle, fashion, and gear — no accent, ink/ivory only). Shop carries merch under whichever division's mark applies. One handle everywhere: @unhingedsocial.us.

**Business:** a global nautical social club. Anchor product is a weekly 7-hour Miami sailing — Trident 512 pontoon, 40 vetted guests, Haulover Sandbar, Shore Leave afterparty. Hard ratio gate at 40 (20/20 or 10 couples + 20 singles). Singles primary, open-minded couples first-class secondary. Full spec in `operations.md`; use the named vocabulary verbatim (Shared Anchor, Chief Vibe Stew, Plot Course, Preference Sheet, Captain's Pass, Captain's Log, Confessional Pod, Intent Wristband, Shore Leave).

**Quick rules:** paper-first greyscale (page #EDEDEA, ink #141414) with one acid-green accent per view (#3EC317); ink dark theme via `data-theme="dark"`. Anton display ≥22px ALL CAPS / Instrument Serif for campaign headlines / Archivo body (400·500·700) / Space Mono uppercase labels and data. Type ladder 9/10/12/14/16/18/22/28/36/48/64. Sub-brands swap accent only (Dating flare #F5256D, Boats cobalt #1D62F0, Shop sun #F07405). Imagery splits: golden-hour 35mm photography for anything with people/water/product; greyscale engraving for type-led surfaces. No emoji, no exclamation marks, no Title Case. Consent and safety copy drops the brand voice — plain and literal. Lucide icons only — never hand-drawn SVGs. No logo exists — use the type-set `Wordmark`: bracketed `[UN]` anchor in caps plus a sentence-case suffix, e.g. [UN] Hinged.
